# Privacy and Retention Proposal

This system processes confidential student and family conversations. Before production use, DPS must approve:

- recording notice and consent language
- who may access recordings and transcripts
- retention duration
- deletion workflow
- legal/privacy basis for processing
- backup retention and deletion limitations
- incident response ownership

The application should retain only required information, avoid sensitive content in logs, and keep audio private. Deletion must cover database records, generated documents, and storage objects subject to the approved retention and backup policy.
